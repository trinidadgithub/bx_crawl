# Monster descriptions for B/X Crawl
# Key format: monster_name_to_key() normalizes "Dragon, Red" -> "dragon-red"
# Multi-line values supported (continuation lines have no '=' sign)

acolyte = Acolytes are 1st level NPC clerics on a pilgrimage to or from a
holy (or unholy) shrine, or perhaps merely seeking adventure. All of the clerics in the party will be of the
same alignment, but the entire party has an equal chance to be Lawful,
Neutral, or Chaotic. The acolytes do not know any spells. If there are 4 or
more encountered they will be led by either a 2nd, 3rd, 4th, or 5th level
cleric (roll 1d10: 1-4 = 2nd level leader, 5-7 = 3rd level, 8-9 = 4th
level, 10 = 5th level). The DM may choose any clerical spells for the
leader, or they may be rolled at random.

ape-white = White apes have lost their color due to many years of living in
caves. They are nocturnal, sleeping in the day and looking for fruits and
vegetables at night. If creatures approach their lair, the apes will
threaten them. If their threats are ignored, they will attack. They may
throw one stone per round for 1d6 damage. White apes are not intelligent
and sometimes are kept as pets by Neanderthals.

bandit = Bandits are NPC thieves who have joined together to rob others.
They will act as normal humans in order to surprise their intended victims.
Treasure Type A is only found when bandits are encountered in their
wilderness lair. Bandits may have an NPC leader of any class, of a higher
experience level than the bandits.

bat-giant = Giant bats are carnivorous (meat-eaters) and may attack a
party if extremely hungry. More dangerous still are giant vampire bats:
5% of all giant bat encounters are vampire bats. Their bite does no
extra damage but the victim must save vs. Paralysis or fall unconscious
for 1-10 rounds, allowing the bat to drain blood (1-4 hp/round). Any
victim who dies from blood loss must save vs. Spells or become undead
24 hours later.

bat-normal = Normal bats will not attack men but may confuse them by
flying around their heads. At least ten bats are needed to confuse one
character. Confused characters suffer -2 on attack rolls and saving
throws and cannot cast spells. Normal bats must check morale each
round unless controlled or summoned.

bear-black = Bears are well known to all adventurers. If a bear (of any
type) hits with both paws on the same victim in one round of combat, the
bear has hugged its victim and will cause 2-16 (2d8) additional points of
damage in the same round as the attack. Black bears have black fur and
stand about 6' tall. They are omnivorous, but prefer roots and berries. A
black bear will not usually attack unless cornered and unable to escape.
Adult black bears fight to the death to protect their young. They have been
known to raid camps seeking food, especially fresh fish and sweets.

bear-cave = Bears are well known to all adventurers. If a bear (of any
type) hits with both paws on the same victim in one round of combat, the
bear has hugged its victim and will cause 2-16 (2d8) additional points of
damage in the same round as the attack. Cave bears are a type of giant
grizzly bear that live in caves and lost world areas. They stand about 15'
tall and are the most ferocious of all bears. Though omnivorous, they
prefer meat and enjoy human flesh. Cave bears have bad eyesight but a good
sense of smell. If hungry, they will follow a trail of blood until they have
eaten.

bear-grizzly = Bears are well known to all adventurers. If a bear (of any
type) hits with both paws on the same victim in one round of combat, the
bear has hugged its victim and will cause 2-16 (2d8) additional points of
damage in the same round as the attack. Grizzly bears have silver-tipped
brown or reddish brown fur and stand about 9' tall. They are fond of meat
and are much more likely to attack than black bears. Grizzlies are found in
most climates, but are most common in mountains and forests.

bear-polar = Bears are well known to all adventurers. If a bear (of any
type) hits with both paws on the same victim in one round of combat, the
bear has hugged its victim and will cause 2-16 (2d8) additional points of
damage in the same round as the attack. Polar bears have white fur and
stand about 11' tall. They live in cold regions and usually eat fish, but
are as likely to attack as grizzly bears. These huge bears are good
swimmers, and their wide feet allow them to run across snow without
sinking.

beetle-fire = Fire beetles are 2 1/2' long creatures often found below
ground. They are nocturnal, but may be active underground at any time.
A fire beetle has two glowing glands above its eyes and one near the back
of its abdomen. These glands give off light in a 10' radius and continue to
glow for 1-6 days after they are removed.

beetle-giant = Giant beetles are large, armoured insects with powerful
mandibles.

beetle-oil = Oil beetles are 3' long giant beetles that sometimes burrow
underground. When attacked, they squirt an oily fluid at one attacker. If
the oil hits, it raises painful blisters, causing a penalty on attack rolls
until cure light wounds is used or 24 hours pass. Oil beetles can also
attack with their mandibles.

beetle-tiger = Tiger beetles are 4' long giant beetles with striped
carapaces that look like tiger skin. They are carnivorous and usually prey
on robber flies, but have been known to attack and eat humans, crushing
them with powerful mandibles.

berserker = Berserkers are simply fighters who go mad in battle. Their
reactions are determined normally, but once a battle starts they will always
fight to the death — sometimes attacking their comrades in their blind rage.
When fighting humans or human-like creatures, such as kobolds, goblins or
orcs, they add +2 to their "to hit" rolls due to this ferocity. They never
retreat, surrender, or take prisoners. Treasure Type B is only found in the
wilderness.

boar = Wild boars generally prefer forested areas but can be found nearly
everywhere. They are omnivorous and have extremely nasty tempers when
disturbed.

bugbear = Bugbears are giant hairy goblins. Despite their size and
awkward walk, they move very quietly and attack without warning
whenever they can. They surprise opponents easily. When using
weapons, they add +1 to all damage rolls due to their strength.

carrion-crawler = This scavenger is worm-shaped, 9' long and 3' high with
many legs. It can move equally well on a floor, wall, or ceiling like a
spider. Its mouth is surrounded by 8 tentacles, each 2' long, which can
paralyze on a successful hit unless a saving throw vs. Paralysis is made.
Once paralyzed, a victim will be eaten (unless the carrion crawler is being
attacked). The paralysis can be removed by a cure light wounds spell, but
any spell so used will have no other effect. Without a spell, the paralysis
will wear off in 2-8 turns.

cat-great = The Great Cats are normally cautious and avoid fights unless
forced by extreme hunger or trapped with no escape. Though they may be
relaxed or playful, they are subject to sudden violent changes of temper.
They sometimes develop a taste for one prey, even humans or human-like
creatures. They rarely go deep into caves, are very inquisitive, and always
chase fleeing prey.

cat-mountain-lion = The Great Cats are normally cautious and avoid fights
unless forced by extreme hunger or trapped with no escape. Though they may
be relaxed or playful, they are subject to sudden violent changes of temper.
They sometimes develop a taste for one prey, even humans or human-like
creatures. They rarely go deep into caves, are very inquisitive, and always
chase fleeing prey. Mountain lions are tawny-furred Great Cats that live
mostly in mountainous regions but also inhabit forests and deserts. They
will wander farther into dungeons than any other species of Great Cat.

cat-panther = The Great Cats are normally cautious and avoid fights unless
forced by extreme hunger or trapped with no escape. Though they may be
relaxed or playful, they are subject to sudden violent changes of temper.
They sometimes develop a taste for one prey, even humans or human-like
creatures. They rarely go deep into caves, are very inquisitive, and always
chase fleeing prey. Panthers are found on plains, forests, and open
shrublands. They are extremely quick and can outrun most prey over short
distances.

cat-lion = The Great Cats are normally cautious and avoid fights unless
forced by extreme hunger or trapped with no escape. Though they may be
relaxed or playful, they are subject to sudden violent changes of temper.
They sometimes develop a taste for one prey, even humans or human-like
creatures. They rarely go deep into caves, are very inquisitive, and always
chase fleeing prey. Lions generally live in warm climates, and thrive in
savannah and brush lands near deserts. They usually hunt in groups known
as prides.

cat-tiger = The Great Cats are normally cautious and avoid fights unless
forced by extreme hunger or trapped with no escape. Though they may be
relaxed or playful, they are subject to sudden violent changes of temper.
They sometimes develop a taste for one prey, even humans or human-like
creatures. They rarely go deep into caves, are very inquisitive, and always
chase fleeing prey. Tigers are the largest of the commonly found Great
Cats. They prefer cooler climates and wooded lands where their striped
bodies offer camouflage when hunting. They surprise prey on a 1-4 on 1d6
when in woods.

cat-sabre-tooth-tiger = The Great Cats are normally cautious and avoid
fights unless forced by extreme hunger or trapped with no escape. Though
they may be relaxed or playful, they are subject to sudden violent changes
of temper. They sometimes develop a taste for one prey, even humans or
human-like creatures. They rarely go deep into caves, are very inquisitive,
and always chase fleeing prey. Sabre-tooth tigers are the largest and most
ferocious of the Great Cats. They have oversized fangs, from which they
get their name. Fortunately, sabre-tooth tigers are mostly extinct, except
in lost world areas.

cat-wild = Wild cats are stealthy forest predators that stalk prey before
springing from concealment. They prefer rabbits, deer, and other small
game, but hungry specimens may attack lone travelers. They avoid large
groups whenever possible. This category includes medium-sized cats such as
bobcats, lynxes, ocelots, caracals, servals, jungle cats, fishing cats,
leopard cats, margays, pampas cats, Pallas's cats, African wildcats,
European wildcats, and sand cats. A wild cat gains a +2 bonus to surprise
rolls when hiding in woods, brush, or rocky terrain.

cave-locust = Cave locusts are 2-3' long, stone gray, giant grasshoppers
that live underground. Because of their color they may not be noticed or
may be mistaken for a statue until closely approached. They are herbivorous
(eating plants) and also eat fungus such as yellow mold and shriekers.
Because of this they cannot be harmed by yellow mold. They are also not
harmed by most poisons. They are very nervous and will flee most of the
time rather than fight. They flee by jumping up to 60' away. Unfortunately
their sense of direction is not too good, and they frequently accidentally
jump into a party. If they jump towards the party (50% chance) a character
is determined randomly and a "to hit" roll is made. If the locust hits a
character, that character takes 1-4 points of damage from being battered by
the insect. The locust will then fly away. When frightened or attacked cave
locusts make a loud shrieking noise to warn their fellows. This shriek has
a 20% chance per round of attracting wandering monsters to investigate. If
cornered, a cave locust will spit a brown gooey substance up to 10'. To hit
a character the locust needs to make an attack against Armor Class 9. A
character hit by cave locust spittle must save vs. Poison or be unable to
do anything for 1 turn due to the awful smell. After this time he or she
will be used to the smell, but any character approaching within 5' of him or
her must also save or be violently ill. This effect will last until the
spittle is washed off.

centipede-giant = Giant centipedes are insects with 1' long bodies and
many legs. They like damp, dark places. Their bite does no damage, but the
victim must save vs. Poison or become violently ill for 10 days. Characters
who fail move at half speed and cannot perform other physical actions.

doppleganger = These man-sized, shape-changing creatures are
intelligent and evil. A doppleganger can shape itself into the form
of any human-like creature (up to 7' tall) it sees. Its favorite trick
is to kill the original person and take their place, attacking the
party by surprise. Sleep and charm spells do not affect them and
they save as high-level fighters due to their magical nature. When
killed, a doppleganger reverts to its original form.

dragon-black = Dragons are a very old race of huge winged lizards. They
are hatched from eggs, eat meat, love treasure, have Breath Weapons, and
will do everything possible to save their own lives, including surrender.
Black dragons are found in swamps and marshes and breathe acid in a line.

dragon-blue = Dragons are a very old race of huge winged lizards. They
are proud, treasure-loving meat eaters with Breath Weapons, and even the
hungriest dragon may pause to listen to flattery. Blue dragons are found in
deserts and plains and breathe lightning in a line.

dragon-gold = Gold dragons always talk and use spells. They can change
shape and often appear as a human or animal. They may breathe either fire
in a cone or chlorine gas in a cloud, though still only three Breath Weapon
uses per day. Lawful dragons may help a truly worthy party.

dragon-green = Dragons are a very old race of huge winged lizards. They
are hatched from eggs, eat meat, love treasure, have Breath Weapons, and
will do everything possible to save their own lives, including surrender.
Green dragons are found in jungles and forests and breathe chlorine gas in
a cloud.

dragon-red = Dragons are a very old race of huge winged lizards. They are
proud, treasure-loving meat eaters with Breath Weapons, and even the
hungriest dragon may pause to listen to flattery. Red dragons are found in
mountains and hills and breathe fire in a cone.

dragon-white = Dragons are a very old race of huge winged lizards. They
are hatched from eggs, eat meat, love treasure, have Breath Weapons, and
will do everything possible to save their own lives, including surrender.
White dragons are found in cold regions and breathe cold in a cone.

driver-ant = Driver ants are giant black ants about 6' long. They are
omnivorous and, when hungry, devour anything edible in their path. Once
engaged in combat they fight to the death, even trying to cross flames to
reach opponents. The nest-lair is always guarded, and legends say driver
ants mine gold; some lairs contain gold nuggets.

dwarf = Dwarves may appear as NPC clan groups, war bands, or mining
expeditions. For every 20 dwarves there will be a leader of higher level
who may have magical items. As long as their leader is fighting with them
and alive, dwarven morale is higher. Dwarves hate goblins and usually
attack them on sight.

elf = Elves are slender, graceful demihumans. They can fight as
fighters and cast spells as magic-users. They have infravision 60'
and are resistant to paralysis. They are Neutral.

ferret-giant = Giant ferrets look like 3' long weasels. They hunt giant rats
underground and are sometimes trained for this purpose. Unfortunately,
their tempers are highly unpredictable, and they have been known to attack
their trainers and other humans.

gargoyle = Gargoyles are grotesque, winged stone monsters that
appear to be statues when motionless. They attack with two claws,
a bite, and a horn. They are not affected by sleep or charm spells.
Only magical weapons can hit them.

gelatinous-cube = Gelatinous cubes are 10' cubes of transparent
jelly that wander dungeons, absorbing organic matter. They are
almost invisible, surprising opponents easily. Their touch causes
paralysis (save vs. Paralysis, lasting 2-8 turns unless removed by
cure light wounds). They may be harmed by fire and weapons
but not by cold or lightning.

ghoul = Ghouls are hideous, beast-like undead humans who attack
anything living. Any successful hit by a ghoul will paralyze any
creature of ogre-size or smaller (except elves) unless the victim
saves vs. Paralysis. Once an opponent is paralyzed, the ghoul turns
and attacks another opponent until the ghoul is destroyed or all its
opponents are paralyzed or dead. Paralysis lasts 2-8 turns unless
removed by a cure light wounds spell. Ghouls are immune to sleep and
charm.

giant-ferret = see Ferret, Giant

gnoll = Gnolls are beings of low intelligence that appear to be human-like
hyenas. They may use any weapons. They are strong, but dislike work and
prefer to bully and steal for a living. For every 20 gnolls encountered,
one is a stronger leader. Gnolls are rumored to be the result of a magical
combination of a gnome and a troll by an evil magic-user.

gnome = Gnomes are a human-like race related to dwarves. They are smaller
than dwarves and have long noses and full beards. They have infravision to
90' and usually live in lowland burrows. Gnomes are excellent metalsmiths
and miners, love gold and gems, and are fond of machinery. They like most
dwarves but war with goblins and kobolds, usually attacking kobolds on
sight. Gnome groups and lairs have leaders whose presence raises morale.

goblin = Goblins are a small, incredibly ugly human-like race. Their
skin is a pale earthy color, such as chalky tan or livid gray. Their
eyes are red and glow in the dark like rat's eyes. Goblins live
underground and have well-developed infravision to 90'. In full
daylight they suffer a penalty on attack rolls. Goblins hate dwarves
and will attack them on sight. There is a chance that some goblins
will be riding dire wolves.

green-slime = Green slime looks like green, oozing slime. It can be
harmed by fire or cold, but not by other attacks. It dissolves wood and
metal in 6 rounds, but cannot dissolve stone. It often clings to walls
and ceilings and drops on surprised characters. Once on flesh it sticks
and turns the flesh into more green slime. It cannot be scraped off,
but must be burnt off or treated with cure disease. If it is not burned
off, the victim turns completely into green slime 1-4 rounds after the
first 6-round period. Burning does half damage to the slime and half to
the victim.

halfling = Halflings can appear as NPCs. They live in small villages of
30-300 inhabitants. Each village has a leader and a village guard of militia.
Treasure type B is only found if halflings are encountered in the
wilderness.

hobgoblin = Hobgoblins are bigger and meaner relatives of goblins.
They live underground but often hunt above ground and have no
penalties in full daylight. A hobgoblin king and bodyguards live
in the lair; morale is higher while the king is alive.

killer-bee = Killer bees are 1' long giant bees with a vicious temper. They
almost always attack on sight and automatically attack anyone within 30'
of their underground hive. When a killer bee stings, it dies, but the
victim must save vs. Poison or die. The stinger continues working into the
victim, doing automatic damage each round unless removed. A special honey
in the hive acts as a half-strength potion of healing.

kobold = Kobolds are small, evil dog-like men who live underground.
They have scaly rust-brown skin and no hair. They have well-developed
infravision to 90' and prefer to attack by ambush. A kobold chieftain
and bodyguards live in the lair; morale is higher while the chieftain
is alive. Kobolds hate gnomes and will attack them on sight.

lizard-gecko = Giant geckos are 3-4' long lizards found in warm
climates and dungeons. They are harmless unless provoked. They can
climb walls and ceilings.

lizard-giant-gecko = Giant geckos are large climbing lizards
commonly found in dungeons.

medium = Mediums are NPC 1st level magic-users. There is a 50% chance
that mediums will be accompanied by their master, a 3rd level magic-user.
Each medium has one 1st level spell chosen by the DM or determined
randomly; the master has two 1st level spells and one 2nd level spell.

normal-human = A normal human is a human who does not seek dangerous
adventure and does not have a class. Normal humans have 1 to 4 hit points,
depending on age, health, and profession. Examples include peasants,
children, beggars, villagers, townspeople, slaves, fishermen, scholars, and
workers.

orc = Orcs are ugly human-like creatures who look like a combination of
animal and man. They are nocturnal and prefer to live underground. In
daylight they suffer a penalty on attack rolls. They have bad tempers,
dislike other living things, and often kill for amusement. Orc leaders gain
their positions by defeating others; if a leader is killed, morale drops. Orc
tribes may include ogres, and different tribes are not usually friendly.

pixie = Pixies are small (1-2' tall) human-like creatures with
insect-like wings, distantly related to elves. They are invisible
unless they want to be seen. Unlike the invisibility spell, pixies
can attack and remain invisible, and always gain surprise when
doing so. Their wings can only support them for 3 turns before
they must rest.

rat-giant = Giant rats are 3' long or more and have gray or black fur. They
are often found in dark dungeon corners and in areas where undead monsters
lurk. Rats eat almost anything and some carry disease; anyone bitten has a
chance of infection, which may kill in 1-6 days or leave the victim sick in
bed for a month. Rats usually avoid humans unless summoned or defending
their lair, are good swimmers, and fear fire.

rock-baboon = Rock baboons are larger versions of normal baboons and are
more intelligent. They eat anything but prefer meat. They do not make tools
or weapons but will pick up bones or branches to use as clubs. Rock baboons
form packs led by a dominant male, are ferocious and vicious-tempered, and
communicate warnings and needs with simple screams.

shadow = Shadows are incorporeal ghost-like intelligent creatures.
They look like real shadows and can alter their shape slightly.
They are hard to see and surprise on a 1-5 on 1d6. They can only
be hit by magical weapons. If a shadow scores a hit, it drains 1
point of Strength in addition to doing damage, lasting for 8 turns. Any
creature reduced to 0 Strength becomes a shadow. Shadows are not undead
and cannot be Turned by clerics. They are not affected by sleep or charm
spells.

skeleton = Skeletons are animated undead creatures often found
near graveyards, dungeons, or other deserted places. They are used
as guards by the high level magic-user or cleric who animated
them. Since they are undead, they can be Turned by a cleric. They are
not affected by sleep or charm spells, nor any form of mind reading.
Skeletons will always fight until killed.

snake-cobra = A spitting cobra is a 3' long grayish-white snake that can
squirt venom up to 6 feet, aiming for the victim's eyes. A hit requires a
save vs. Poison or the victim is blinded. Like most small poisonous snakes,
it will not attack human-sized or larger opponents unless startled or
threatened. It can either spit or bite in one round, but usually spits.

snake-pit-viper = A pit viper is a 5' long greenish-gray poisonous snake
with small pits in its head. These pits act as heat sensors with a range of
60'. The combination of pits and infravision makes it very hard to fight a
pit viper; it is so quick that it always gains initiative. Any victim bitten
must save vs. Poison or die.

spider-crab = Crab spiders are 5' long giant spiders. They are meat-eaters
and attack by clinging to walls or ceilings and dropping onto victims. They
have chameleon-like powers and can change color to blend into their
surroundings, surprising on a 1-4 on 1d6. After the first attack they can
be seen normally. Their poison is weak, giving victims a bonus on the save.

spider-giant = Giant spiders are 8-10' long spiders that dwell in
dark places. Their bite requires a save vs. Poison or paralysis sets
in for 1d4 turns. They spin webs to trap prey.

spider-phase = Phase spiders are 6' long magical spiders that can
phase between dimensions. They can appear and disappear at will,
making them extremely dangerous. Their bite requires a save vs.
Poison or the victim dies.

sprite = Sprites are small winged people about 1 foot tall, related
to pixies and elves. While shy, they are very curious and have a
strange sense of humor. Five sprites acting together can cast one
curse spell as a magical practical joke. Sprites will never cause
death on purpose. They carry tiny weapons tipped with sleep
poison, can detect alignment, and become invisible at will.

stirge = A stirge is a birdlike creature with a long nose, looking
rather like a very small feathered anteater. When a stirge attacks,
it tries to thrust its beak into the victim's body to suck out blood
for 1-3 points of damage.
A successful hit means it has attached itself; it will automatically
suck blood for 1-3 points of damage each round until either it or its
victim is dead, then it will leave. A flying stirge gains a +2 bonus on
its first attack roll against any one opponent due to its speed.

troglodyte = Troglodytes are intelligent human-like reptiles with
a short tail, long legs, and a spiny comb on their head and arms.
They walk upright, use their hands as well as humans, hate most other
creatures, and try to kill anyone they meet. They have a chameleon-like
ability to change color and hide by rock walls, surprising on a 1-4 on
1d6. They secrete an oily stench that nauseates humans and demi-humans
unless they save vs. Poison, causing a -2 penalty on attack rolls while
in hand-to-hand combat with troglodytes.

wight = A wight is an undead spirit living in the body of a dead human or
demi-human. It can only be hit by silvered or magical weapons. Wights are
greatly feared because they drain life energy when striking a victim. Each
hit drains one level of experience or hit die; the victim keeps only enough
experience points to be at the midpoint of the lower level and loses one hit
die of hit points. Anyone totally drained by a wight becomes a wight in 1-4
days under the control of the wight who drained them.

wolf = Wolves are meat-eaters and hunt in packs. Though they prefer the
wilderness, they are occasionally found in caves. Captured wolf cubs can be
trained like dogs if the DM permits, but this is difficult. Small groups or
packs reduced below half their original number have lower morale.

wraith = Wraiths are incorporeal undead that drain life energy.
They cannot be hit by normal weapons. Their touch drains one level
of experience. They are immune to sleep, charm, and poison.

zombie = Zombies are undead humans or demi-humans animated by some evil
cleric or magic-user. As undead, they may be Turned by a cleric but are not
affected by sleep or charm spells or any form of mind reading. They are
often placed to guard treasures, since they make no noise until they attack.
Zombies always attack on sight, can be destroyed by normal weapons, and are
slow fighters that always strike last.

giant-shrew = Giant shrews look like brown-furred rats with long snouts.
They can burrow, climb, or jump up to 5'. They are insectivores that hunt
insects, their main food. Their eyes are so weak they are almost blind, and
they use high squeaks and echoes like bats to see underground. A silence
15' radius spell blinds and confuses them. They defend chosen hunting
territories ferociously, always gain initiative on the first attack, and may
frighten weaker creatures into fleeing.

giant-tick = Giant ticks are 2-3' long blood-sucking parasites
that carry debilitating diseases. They attach to victims and
drain blood. Anyone bitten must save vs. Poison or contract a
wasting disease. They can be removed with fire or torn off.

gray-ooze = This seeping horror looks like wet stone and is difficult to
see. It secretes acid that does 2d8 damage if it hits bare skin and can
dissolve and destroy magic armor in one turn. After the first hit, the
ooze sticks to its victim, automatically destroying normal armor and doing
2d8 damage each round. Gray ooze cannot be harmed by cold or fire, but
can be harmed by weapons and lightning.

harpy = A harpy has the lower body of a giant eagle and the upper
body and head of a hideous-looking woman. By their singing,
harpies lure creatures to them to be killed and devoured. Any
creature hearing the song must save vs. Spells or be charmed.
Harpies are resistant to magic and have a bonus on all saves.

living-statue-crystal = A living statue is an enchanted animated creature
made by a powerful wizard. It appears to be a perfectly normal statue until
it moves. Living statues may be of any size or material and are not
affected by sleep spells. Living crystal statues are life forms made of
crystals instead of flesh; they can look like statues of anything, but often
appear human.

living-statue-jade = Jade living statues are magical constructs
immune to sleep and charm. Their touch petrifies (save or die).

living-statue-steel = Steel living statues are enchanted
guardians immune to sleep and charm. Only magical weapons can
hit them.

lizard-giant-horned = Giant horned lizards are 8-10' long
armoured reptiles. They attack with a horn and bite.

lizard-horned = Horned lizards are 4-5' armoured reptiles with
a horn and bite.

lycanthrope-werebear = Werebears are very intelligent lycanthropes
that shift between human, bear, and hybrid forms. They usually
prefer to live alone or with bears but may be friendly if peacefully
approached. In combat, if both paws hit the same target, they may
hug for additional damage. They may summon bears. Only silver
or magical weapons can hit them.

lycanthrope-wereboar = Wereboars are semi-intelligent lycanthropes
with bad tempers. In human form they often act as berserkers,
gaining a bonus to attack and fighting to the death. They summon
normal boars to help in battle. Only silver or magical weapons can
hit them.

lycanthrope-wererat = Wererats are intelligent lycanthropes of
urban areas that can speak Common in either form. They prefer
a man-sized rat form. They are sneaky and often set ambushes,
surprising opponents easily. They summon giant rats to aid them.
Only silver or magical weapons can hit them.

lycanthrope-weretiger = Weretigers are lycanthropes related to
Great Cats. They are curious but dangerous when threatened.
They are good swimmers and quiet trackers, surprising opponents
easily. They may summon Great Cats. Only silver or magical
weapons can hit them.

lycanthrope-werewolf = Werewolves are lycanthropes that are
semi-intelligent and usually hunt in packs. Any group of 5 or
more has a leader. They summon normal wolves to form large
packs. Only silver or magical weapons can hit them.

medusa = A medusa looks like a human female with live snakes growing from
her head instead of hair. The sight of a medusa turns a creature to stone
unless the victim saves vs. Turn to Stone. Her snake bite is poisonous. A
medusa often wears a hooded robe as a disguise. A medusa can be viewed
safely in a mirror, but if she sees her own reflection she may petrify
herself. Attacking without looking is difficult, and a medusa has a bonus
on saves vs. Spells due to her magical nature.

minotaur = Minotaurs are semi-intelligent bull-headed humanoids
that eat humans. They will always attack anything its size or
smaller and pursue as long as prey is in sight. Minotaurs gain +2
to damage with weapons due to strength. They usually live in
tunnels or mazes and never get lost.

mule = A mule is a crossbreed between a horse and a donkey. Mules are
stubborn, and if bothered or excited they may either bite or kick. If the DM
permits it, mules can be taken into dungeons. A mule can carry a normal
load of 2000 coins, or 4000 coins at most with reduced movement. Mules
cannot be trained to attack but will fight in self-defense.

mummy = Mummies are rotting, linen-wrapped undead that guard ancient
tombs and temples. Their touch causes a rotting disease (save vs.
Disease) that prevents all magical healing; only a cure disease
spell can remove it. They are immune to normal weapons and take
half damage from magical weapons. Fire deals double damage.

noble = Noble is a general term for the lord of a castle and any relatives.
In the Basic rules, a noble is always a 3rd level fighter, though the DM may
choose any class and level. A noble always has a squire and may be
accompanied by retainers or hirelings, usually 1st level fighters.

ochre-jelly = An ochre jelly is an ochre-colored giant amoeba. It can
only be harmed by fire or cold. Attacks with weapons or lightning make
it split into 1d4+1 smaller 2 Hit Dice jellies. It does 2d6 damage per
turn to exposed flesh, and the smaller jellies do half damage. It can
seep through small cracks and destroys wood, leather, and cloth in 1
round, but cannot eat through metal or stone.

ogre = Ogres are huge fearsome human-like creatures, usually 8 to
10 feet tall. They wear animal skins for clothes and often live in
caves. When encountered outside their lair they will be carrying
gold. Ogres hate Neanderthals and will attack them on sight.

owl-bear = An owl bear is a huge bear-like creature with the head of a
giant owl. It stands 8' tall and weighs 1500 pounds. Owl bears have nasty
tempers and are usually hungry, preferring meat. If both paws hit the same
opponent in one round, the owl bear hugs for extra damage. They are common
underground and in dense forests.

rust-monster = A rust monster has a body like a giant armadillo
with a long tail and two long front feelers. If a character hits one,
or if it hits with its antennae, any metal armour or weapons
touching it instantly rust and become unusable. Magical items
lose one plus each time they are struck.

shrieker = Shriekers look like giant mushrooms. They live in underground
caverns and move slowly. They react to light within 60' and movement within
30' by emitting a piercing shriek for 1-3 rounds. Each round of shrieking
has a chance to attract a wandering monster, which arrives later.

spider-tarantella = A tarantella is a huge hairy magical spider that looks
like a 7' long tarantula. Its bite does not kill; a failed save vs. Poison
causes painful spasms resembling a frantic dance. Anyone watching must save
vs. Spells or dance too. Dancing victims suffer attack penalties, attackers
gain bonuses against them, and exhausted dancers become helpless.

thoul = A thoul is a magical combination of a ghoul, a hobgoblin, and a
troll. Except when very close, thouls look exactly like hobgoblins and are
sometimes part of a hobgoblin king's bodyguard. Their touch paralyses like
a ghoul's, and while alive they regenerate damage each round.

trader = Traders are first level fighters who make their living trading
goods. They are similar to merchants but much braver and better fighters.
They usually carry swords and hand axes, wear furs treated as leather
armor, and carry shields. In the wilderness they lead pack mules carrying
trade goods such as spices, furs, or carved decorative items.

veteran = Veterans are low-level fighters, usually returning from or going
to war. Each veteran's level and alignment may be determined as for an NPC
party. A party of veterans may have mixed levels and alignments, or all
members may share the same level.
