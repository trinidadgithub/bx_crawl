# Phase 31 - Undead Drain and Weapon Immunity

Shadows, wights, and wraiths fought with generic melee in the procedural
dungeons. B/X gives them two signature rules: a touch that drains (wight and
wraith drain one level; the shadow drains 1 point of Strength), and immunity to
weapons that lack the right material (wights: only silver or magical weapons
hit them; shadows and wraiths: only magical weapons). This slice adds both
rules where the rest of the special-attack system lives, without touching the
auto-generated `bx_data.hpp` tables.

## Data at spawn (name predicates, as elsewhere)

The monster table is generated by `scripts/gen_data.py`, so special abilities
continue to be set at spawn from the monster name — exactly how `undead`,
`has_poison`, `has_disease`, and `has_paralysis` already work. Two new flags and
one material tier ride `Entity`:

- `drains_level` — wight and wraith touches drain one hero level.
- `drains_strength` — the shadow's touch drains 1 STR.
- `WeaponImmunity` (`None` / `SilverOrMagic` / `MagicOnly`) — wights are
  `SilverOrMagic`; shadows and wraiths are `MagicOnly`.

`spawn_bx_monster_by_index` derives all three the same way it derives the other
flags. No save-format change is needed: hero level, STR, HP, attack bonus,
damage bonus, and weapon id already ride save v7.

## Level drain

On a wight/wraith touch that hits and leaves the hero alive, `apply_level_drain`
mirrors `check_level_up` inverted:

- the hero drops one level (never below level 1 — a first-level hero's touch
  still deals its hit-point damage but the drain itself has no effect);
- one hit die plus the CON modifier comes off max HP (floor 1), and current HP
  clamps into the new maximum;
- Fighters have their attack bonus recomputed from `fighter_attack_bonus_for_level`
  plus the STR adjustment; other classes lose exactly the +1 the level-up granted;
- spell slots re-grant from the class table at the drained level;
- XP falls to the midpoint of the drained level's band, so the next kill cannot
  immediately return the level.

`make_save` is deliberately not involved: B/X energy drain has no saving throw.

## Strength drain

The shadow's touch reduces `hero.abilities.str` by one (floor 3). Because `Game`
always stores the STR component inside `attack_bonus` and `damage_bonus`, the
drain just applies the delta of the STR to-hit adjustment to both fields, for
every class, including recomputed Fighters.

## Weapon immunity

`hero_weapon_can_wound(g, immunity)` asks whether the hero's currently equipped
weapon can wound a monster of a given tier. The only silver weapon in the item
tables is `silver-dagger` (resolved by `bx::weapon_index`, not a string guess);
there is no magical weapon item yet, so `MagicOnly` monsters (shadows, wraiths)
cannot be wounded by melee at all — which is accurate B/X, and those monsters
remain killable through spells, thrown holy water, and cleric turning, all of
which bypass the melee gate exactly as before.

The gate is applied in both directions hero-to-monster attacks flow:

- `attack_internal` (melee/backstab bumps): a hit that lands on an immune
  monster logs "The <name> is unharmed by your weapon!" and deals no damage and
  no morale check; the attack still consumes the turn.
- `ranged_attack` (thrown knives/darts): the same gate protects thrown
  missiles; ammo is spent but the monster takes nothing.

Monster-to-hero attacks are unaffected (a monster's touch is not a weapon).

## Tests

- `Wight/wraith/shadow drain traits and weapon immunity are data-driven at
  spawn` — a spawned wight has `drains_level` + `SilverOrMagic`, a wraith has
  `drains_level` + `MagicOnly`, a shadow has `drains_strength` + `MagicOnly`,
  and a goblin has none of either.
- `Wight and wraith touches drain one hero level`:
  - a Cleric 3 drained to level 2 loses a hit die (+CON) of max HP, the
    non-Fighter attack point, both spell slot tiers, and lands at the midpoint
    of the level-2 XP band (1500-3000 → 2250), with the drain logged;
  - a Fighter 7 drained to level 6 has a recomputed attack bonus (+5 band to +2
    band, STR flat);
  - a level-1 hero takes the touch damage but cannot be drained below level 1
    (max HP untouched, still alive, "first-level hero" logged).
- `Shadow touch drains Strength and its melee bonuses` — STR 18 → 17 drops both
  `attack_bonus` and `damage_bonus` by the STR adjustment, and STR 3 cannot drop
  further.
- `Shadow/wight/wraith can be hit only by certain weapons` — a normal sword and
  an unarmed fist can't wound a wight (hp unchanged, "unharmed by your weapon"
  logged), a silver dagger wounds it, a silver dagger still can't wound shadows
  or wraiths, and thrown knives can't wound an immune monster.

Known, intentionally untouched: shadows remain flagged `is_name_undead` and can
be turned / harmed by holy water, although the B/X text describes them as "not
undead". That is a separate data-accuracy question from drain and weapon
immunity; it is noted for a future pass.
